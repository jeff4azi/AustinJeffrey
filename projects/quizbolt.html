Own product

QuizBolt

A university-focused CBT and study platform built around helping students prepare for exams.

Visit quizbolt.site ↗

The problem
Students needed a better way to practise for university exams.
The idea
A CBT-style practice platform shaped around how university exams actually work.
The build
CBT practice, theory questions, flashcards, answer review, bookmarks, retakes, premium access, referrals, leaderboards, payment and access systems, across multiple universities. I worked on the product itself, not just its frontend.
The thinking
We chose a focused university exam-prep experience over a broad all-in-one education platform, because CBT practice, answer review, retakes, flashcards and premium access needed to be genuinely useful to students before expanding further.
The result
QuizBolt has grown to over 1,000 users and has generated real revenue through premium access. It is a working product with students actively using it.
