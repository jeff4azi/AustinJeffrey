Own product

StayBolt

A rental platform, taken from idea to a working product.

Visit stay-bolt.vercel.app ↗

The problem
Finding and renting spaces should be easier.
The idea
Turn the idea of a rental platform into something functional that real people could actually use.
The build
A functioning rental platform.
  • Search for available rental spaces
  • Browse property listings
  • View individual property details
  • See relevant information before deciding
  • Connect renters with property owners
The flow runs from discovering a space to making an enquiry.
The thinking
We chose to build a functional rental platform around the real process of discovering spaces and connecting renters with owners, over stopping at a concept or mockup. The product is live and is still being improved.
The result
StayBolt is live, but still at an early stage of adoption. I am actively improving it and pushing it, with the goal of growing its usefulness and user activity over time.
