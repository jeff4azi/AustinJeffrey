PrepUniv

A marketplace for university-specific quizzes.

Visit prepuniv.com ↗

The problem
Good course-specific study material existed, but students had no place to find it and contributors had no way to earn from it.
The idea
Students discover and purchase course-specific quizzes created by lecturers, top students, HOCs, and other contributors.
The build
A quiz marketplace with creator profiles, quiz previews, a wallet and top-up system, one-time purchases, instant results, and creator monetization.
