Own product

PrepUniv

A marketplace for university-specific quizzes.

Visit prepuniv.com ↗

The problem
Good course-specific study material existed, but students had no place to find it and contributors had no way to earn from it.
The idea
Students discover and purchase course-specific quizzes created by lecturers, top students, HOCs, and other contributors.
The build
A quiz marketplace with creator profiles, quiz previews, a wallet and top-up system, one-time purchases, instant results, and creator monetization.
The thinking
We chose a marketplace where students buy individual course-specific quizzes over putting all content behind a general subscription. It lets lecturers, HOCs and other creators contribute their own quizzes, and gives students exactly what they need.
The result
PrepUniv is in its early stage. The focus now is getting the marketplace, quiz content and creator side in place so it can grow over time.
